2Found a bug?

Send the details to contact@matchmallow.in. You can also open a public GitHub issue if you prefer.

3Your privacy matters

For anything private, email us instead of posting publicly. GitHub issues are public, so please don't share personal information or private quiz answers there.

4Help us help you

For a bug report, include the page you visited, your device and browser, and what happened versus what you expected. Avoid sending passwords or sensitive information.
