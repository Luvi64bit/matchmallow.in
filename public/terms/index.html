About Matchmallow

Matchmallow is a playful compatibility quiz for entertainment. It is not a scientific assessment, relationship recommendation, or professional advice.

Age requirement

The quiz is intended for adults aged 18 or older. The current form asks you to confirm your age; server-side verification has not yet been implemented.

Respectful use

Do not use the site to harass, impersonate, threaten, or collect information about others without permission. Do not submit sensitive information as a nickname.

Prototype limitations

The creator quiz currently runs in your browser. Share links, stored matches, deletion controls, and recipient results are not yet available. Features and content may change during development.
