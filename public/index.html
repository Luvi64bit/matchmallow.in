How it works

Matchmallow is a compatibility quiz you send to your partner, crush or bestie as a link. One of you starts it, the other finishes it, and you both see the match.

  1. Make your card

    Answer 10 quick, silly questions about how you love, text and plan your weekends.

  2. Get your link

    Your personal link is ready right away.

    Example link matchmallow.in/v/piper-8x2

  3. Send it

    Drop it in WhatsApp, Instagram or wherever you and your person talk.

  4. See the match

    They answer the same 10 questions. You both get a score and three highlights.

Good questions

Is it free?

Yes. Making your vibe card, sending your link and seeing your match are all free.

Do I need an account?

No. Pick a nickname, answer the questions and you get your link. The person you send it to doesn’t need an account either.

Who can see my answers?

Only you. The other person sees your score and three highlights, never your actual answers.

How is the score worked out?

Each question has a weight from 1 to 3, and love language and conflict style count most. The same answer earns full points, a complementary pair like planner and spontaneous earns half. Your score is the points you earned out of the points possible.

Can I delete my link?

Yes, any time. Once your link is deleted, nobody can open it or answer it.

Is it for adults?

Yes. Matchmallow is made for people 18 and over, and we ask you to confirm your age before you start.
