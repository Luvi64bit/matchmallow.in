Updated 9 November 2026 · Prototype information

1What you enter

The current creator quiz asks for a nickname, confirmation that you are 18 or older, and ten answers.

2Where your answers go

In the current prototype, your quiz answers are held in the open page's memory. The quiz does not submit them to a Matchmallow application server or create a stored share link. Refreshing or leaving the page may erase your progress.

3Website hosting

As with other websites, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser details. The exact hosting logs, retention periods, and any third-party services must be confirmed before this becomes a final privacy policy.

4Sharing and deletion

Persistent quiz sharing, result storage, and link deletion are not yet available. Do not enter sensitive personal information in your nickname.

5Policy status

This page describes the current prototype and is not a finalized privacy policy. The full policy will be updated before data storage or public launch is enabled.
